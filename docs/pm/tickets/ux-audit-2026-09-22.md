# Ticket: ux-audit-2026-09-22

**Change:** Fix the code-fixable findings from the Sept 22, 2026 UX audit
(`docs/design/panache-storefront-ux-audit-2026-09-22.md`): mobile nav has no
account/login entry point (F1), product gallery thumbnails don't work — being
replaced with a swipeable carousel + working thumbnail strip (F3), category
tile text has no legibility guard against its background photo (F4), checkout
confirmation copy overstates order status in WhatsApp mode (F5), required/
optional field labeling is inconsistent across forms (F6), pagination doesn't
scroll results into view and there are no visible breadcrumbs (F7), and there's
no branded 404/error page (F8). Privacy/Terms (F2) gets clearly-labeled draft
placeholder copy for legal review, not final content.

**Why:** Surfaced by a full-site UI/UX review; F1 and F2 were rated P0
(account/orders unreachable on mobile; legal pages still stub while checkout
collects PII), F3–F5 rated P1, F6–F8 rated P2.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- A signed-out or signed-in visitor on a viewport below 640px can reach
  Login/My Account/My Orders (and Log Out, when signed in) from the mobile
  menu without going through checkout first.
- On the product detail page, clicking/tapping any thumbnail displays that
  image full-size; the gallery also responds to a horizontal swipe/drag
  gesture and to left/right arrow keys.
- Homepage and shop category tiles keep body-text-level contrast against
  their background photo regardless of the photo's own brightness.
- Order confirmation copy in WhatsApp checkout mode no longer states the
  order was "received" before the WhatsApp message is actually sent.
- Checkout's guest fields and the shared auth form both mark non-required
  fields "(optional)" using the same mechanism.
- Changing pages in product/category grids and account orders scrolls the
  results back into view; Shop, Shop/[category], and Product pages show a
  visible breadcrumb trail matching the existing JSON-LD.
- An unknown route renders a branded "page not found" screen instead of the
  Next.js default; an uncaught render error renders a branded recovery
  screen with a retry action.
- `/privacy` and `/terms` render real draft policy/terms content, visibly
  marked as a draft pending legal review, instead of "Coming Soon."

**Risk flag:** No. UI/content-only; no schema, auth, or infra changes.
Design_stage is NOT skipped — several items are visual/UX changes, so a
lightweight design ticket accompanies this one.
