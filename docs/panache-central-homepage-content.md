# Panache Central — Homepage Content

Voice, palette, and locked copy pulled from Brand Identity Guide v1.0. Structure follows standard homepage flow: hero, positioning, benefits, presentation, collection teaser, trust, objections, final CTA, footer.

---

## 1. Hero (Above the Fold)

**Headline (recommended):**
> Fine Jewelry. Made to Last.

**Subheadline:**
> Each piece is chosen, not stocked, to sit beside gold and diamonds without looking out of place.

**Primary CTA:**
> Shop the Collection

**Annotation:** The headline is your locked tagline verbatim, used as-is rather than rewritten, since the guide names it permanent. The subheadline pulls directly from the positioning statement in Section 1 of the brand guide. "Shop the Collection" beats a generic "Shop Now" because it names the destination, per the CTA formula (action + what they get).

**Alternatives:**
- **Option A** — "Fine Jewelry. Made to Last." — Direct, uses the locked tagline, safest choice for consistency.
- **Option B** — "Panache Central. Jewelry, Not Trends." — The other approved tagline, more combative, works better if the brand wants to lead with a point of view rather than a promise.
- **Option C** — "Chosen. Not Stocked." — Pulls the sharpest phrase from the positioning statement itself, shortest and most quotable, but breaks from using an approved tagline verbatim.

*(This drives what the scroll-reveal hero visual sits behind — headline and subhead overlay the pinned sequence, CTA sits below it once the sequence completes.)*

## 2. Positioning Statement

**Section header:**
> A House, Not a Page

**Body copy (locked, verbatim from the brand guide):**
> Panache Central is a fine jewelry house for people who buy once and keep forever. Every piece is chosen — not stocked — to sit alongside gold and diamonds without looking out of place.

**Annotation:** This is copied exactly from Section 1 of the guide rather than paraphrased. It's the sentence every future product and page gets tested against, so it earns a direct placement on the homepage rather than a rewritten version.

## 3. Why Panache Central (Benefits)

**Section header:**
> Built to Outlast the Occasion

**Pillar 1 — Chosen, Not Stocked**
> Each design earns its place in the collection on its own. Nothing goes out under the Panache Central name to fill a gap in a catalog.

**Pillar 2 — One Material Standard**
> Stainless steel, moissanite, and gold pieces are held to the same bar. There's no lower tier and no starter line.

**Pillar 3 — Made for the Long Wear**
> Pieces are built for daily wear and for handing down, not for one event and a drawer.

**Pillar 4 — Presentation Included**
> Every order arrives boxed and carded, not bagged. See Section 4.

**Annotation:** Three pillars would be tighter, but the fourth (presentation) sets up Section 4 below and gives the packaging system a reason to exist in the copy, not just in the warehouse.
